90-161.Z

Publications and Forms Management

1open task

Track and manage DAF publications through the DAFMAN 90-161 publishing process at wing/installation level: status tracking, document cleanup and formatting, and endorsement routing. See who has each publication or form, which endorsements are in, and what is due next. The Administrator works in one window on the work computer (first test in R41); AOs and endorsers will answer in SharePoint forms from an e-mail link (planned). It runs in your wing's own SharePoint site and Power Automate, and RADE will install it (in progress).

By the book:
every stage and format rule cites its DAFMAN 90-161 paragraph; each time limit comes from DAFMAN 90-161 or a recorded Administrator decision.
Endorsements:
flows make the endorsement rows and send the reminders and escalations; the answer path for endorsers (first test in R41) and the optional wave (planned).
System of record:
endorsements are kept here, not in TMT.
Clean drafts:
any draft becomes a correctly formatted final; the format comes from code, never from an AI model. (planned)
Reviews:
periodic reviews open on their own; special reviews, and a rescind action when no answer comes by the due date (planned).
Boundary:
nothing leaves DoD365 and GenAI.mil, except the Administrator's round results e-mail until the pilot starts.
Access:
your own account; standard connectors; no tenant admin, no service account.
Install:
in progress: one paste in RADE for the scripts and lists, the flows imported by hand, and a tested fallback for each step that needs extra rights.
Verifiable:
read the commented code as plain text before you paste it.

Have concerns? Ask in the Teams channel RHOPE-OPS.
