90-161.Z

Publications and Forms Management

Track and manage DAF publications through the DAFMAN 90-161 publishing process at wing/installation level: status tracking, document cleanup and formatting, and endorsement routing. See who has each publication or form, which endorsements are in, and what is due next. The PFM works in one window on the work computer; AOs and endorsers will answer in SharePoint forms from an e-mail link (planned). It runs in your wing's own SharePoint site and Power Automate, and RADE installs it.

By the book:
every stage and format rule cites its DAFMAN 90-161 paragraph; each time limit comes from DAFMAN 90-161 or a recorded PFM decision.
Endorsements:
flows make the endorsement rows and send the reminders and escalations; the answer path for endorsers and the optional wave (planned).
System of record:
endorsements are kept here, not in TMT.
Clean drafts:
any draft becomes a correctly formatted final; the format comes from code, never from an AI model. (planned)
Reviews:
periodic and special reviews start on their own; no answer by the due date opens a rescind action. (planned)
Boundary:
nothing leaves DoD365 and GenAI.mil, except the PFM's round results e-mail until the pilot starts.
Access:
your own account; standard connectors; no tenant admin, no service account.
Install:
the scripts and lists through one paste in RADE; the flows are imported by hand; a tested fallback for each step that needs extra rights (in progress).
Verifiable:
read the commented code as plain text before you paste it.
